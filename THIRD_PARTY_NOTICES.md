# Third-party notices

## Agent notification sounds

The four notification sounds in `apps/web/public/sounds/` are selected from
the **UI Sound Effects (Button Clicks, User Feedback, Notifications)** pack by
Robin Lamb, available at:

<https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications>

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). The upstream pack notes
that its source samples are from the public-domain VCSL and VSCO 2 CE libraries.

Included files and source names:

- `agent-completed.ogg` — `chimes.ogg`
- `plan-ready.ogg` — `Ding.ogg`
- `input-required.ogg` — `alarm.ogg`
- `agent-failed.ogg` — `negative_sound.ogg`
